Electric Vehicle Sales and Market Growth Dashboard using Tableau

This project presents a detailed Tableau dashboard that analyzes global Electric Vehicle sales and market expansion. As the transition toward sustainable mobility accelerates, EV adoption has become a major indicator of economic, technological, and environmental progress. This dashboard provides a structured view of EV sales trends, market share evolution, and growth patterns across regions and time periods.

Project Overview

The primary objective of this dashboard is to deliver a data driven perspective on how the EV industry is evolving. It consolidates key metrics such as sales volume, year over year growth, regional contributions, and adoption trends. The visualizations are designed to support strategic decisions related to market forecasting, investment planning, sustainability initiatives, and policy development.

Objectives

Provide a clear understanding of Electric Vehicle sales patterns
Track year over year performance and long term adoption trends
Identify the regions driving EV market growth
Highlight the impact of global sustainability and technological advancement
Enable business users to explore interactive and comparative visual insights

Dataset Description

The dataset includes information on
Total EV sales across multiple years
Region wise distribution and market share
Growth percentages and adoption trends
Category wise breakdown such as Battery Electric Vehicles and Plug in Hybrid Electric Vehicles

All data points were cleaned, validated, and structured before importing into Tableau.

Dashboard Features

Sales Performance Analysis
Time series visualizations showcasing EV demand and industry expansion.

Growth and Adoption Trends
Year wise growth indicators, CAGR insights, and international market penetration.

Regional Market Comparison
Comparative insights across countries and regions to identify high growth markets.

Market Share Visualization
Share of different EV categories and the contribution of leading markets.

Interactive Filters
Dynamic selection based on year, region, category, and vehicle type to enhance decision making.

Tools and Technologies

Tableau Desktop for dashboard development
Excel or CSV files as primary data sources
Data preprocessing and validation for accuracy

Key Insights

EV sales have shown consistent and significant growth across consecutive years.
Certain regions demonstrate rapid adoption driven by government incentives, infrastructure expansion, and technological innovation.
Market share of Battery Electric Vehicles is increasing steadily as consumers shift toward zero emission transportation solutions.
Global trends indicate that the EV industry is entering a sustained growth phase supported by environmental priorities and industry investments.

How to Use

Download the Tableau TWBX file from the repository.
Open it in Tableau Desktop.
Use filters and interactive elements to explore patterns, compare markets, and analyze trends.
